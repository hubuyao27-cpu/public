# Hierarchical Mesoporous Ni-Fe Oxyhydroxide Catalysts for Alkaline Oxygen Evolution: A Synthetic Benchmark Paper

Title: Hierarchical Mesoporous Ni-Fe Oxyhydroxide Catalysts for Alkaline Oxygen Evolution
Authors: Lin Wei; Marie-Claire Dupont; Kenji Sato
Year: 2025
Journal: Journal of Synthetic Energy Catalysis
Version: v1.2
DOI: 10.9999/jsec.2025.0042

## Abstract

We report a hierarchical mesoporous nickel-iron oxyhydroxide electrocatalyst (Meso-NiFeOOH) synthesized via silica-templated hydrothermal growth for the alkaline oxygen evolution reaction (OER). In 1.0 M KOH at 298 K, Meso-NiFeOOH requires an overpotential of 218 ± 4 mV to achieve 10 mA cm^-2, outperforming commercial IrO2 (312 ± 6 mV) by 94 mV. However, increasing the Fe doping ratio above 25 at.% reduces electrical conductivity and increases charge-transfer resistance.

## 1. Introduction

Previous studies have reported that Fe incorporation into NiOOH lattices lowers the OER overpotential through Fe-O-Ni dual-site stabilization [1, 2]. However, excessive Fe segregation at high anodic potentials may promote phase separation into poorly conductive Fe2O3 domains, although direct in-situ confirmation remains tentative.

## 2. Materials and Methods

Meso-NiFeOOH catalysts were derived from a mesoporous SBA-15 silica hard template followed by selective NaOH etching at 353 K for 12 h. Electrochemical measurements were evaluated on a rotating disk electrode (RDE, 1600 rpm) in O2-saturated 1.0 M KOH at 298 K with iR-compensation (95%). Structural characterization used nitrogen physisorption (BET), transmission electron microscopy (TEM), and X-ray photoelectron spectroscopy (XPS).

## 3. Results and Discussion

In 1.0 M KOH at 298 K, Meso-NiFeOOH achieves a current density of 10 mA cm^-2 at an overpotential of 218 ± 4 mV. At 10 mA cm^-2, Meso-NiFeOOH exhibits a Tafel slope of 34.2 mV dec^-1, which is lower than that of commercial IrO2 (56.8 mV dec^-1). Nitrogen physisorption confirms that Meso-NiFeOOH possesses a Brunauer-Emmett-Teller (BET) specific surface area of 142 m^2 g^-1 and a narrow mesopore diameter centered at 6.4 nm.

Chronopotentiometry at 100 mA cm^-2 for 120 h showed that Meso-NiFeOOH retains 96.5% of its initial potential efficiency without measurable Fe dissolution (< 0.2 ppm by ICP-OES). Addition of 0.1 mM FeCl3 to the electrolyte further increased the turnover frequency (TOF) of pure NiOOH by 4.3-fold at 1.50 V vs. RHE. Conversely, Meso-NiFeOOH showed no statistically significant catalytic activity for the hydrogen evolution reaction (HER) in 1.0 M KOH (overpotential > 480 mV at 10 mA cm^-2, p = 0.42).

When the Fe doping fraction was raised from 15 at.% to 30 at.%, the charge-transfer resistance (Rct) increased from 3.1 Ohm to 11.4 Ohm, and the OER overpotential deteriorated from 218 mV to 285 mV. Complete removal of the SBA-15 silica template is required for optimal pore accessibility, as residual SiO2 blocks active sites. Furthermore, prolonged exposure to CO2-contaminated ambient KOH (pH < 12.5) causes rapid carbonate precipitation and decreases geometric current density by 28% within 6 h. Finally, Meso-NiFeOOH is applicable to anion-exchange membrane water electrolyzers (AEMWEs) operating at 333 K.
